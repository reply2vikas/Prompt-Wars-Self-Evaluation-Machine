    How accurate is this tool?

    Measured only against real results you have recorded. Errors below are held-out: each result is predicted without having seen it.

    What makes it more accurate

    • Anchors. Recorded results are shown to the judge as reference points, so it scores on the real grader's scale.
    • Median of three passes. Removes most of the judge's run-to-run jitter.
    • Calibration. A small correction fitted on your results. It switches on per parameter only when it beats both the raw score and "always guess the average" on held-out data. Run promptwars-score evaluate then promptwars-score calibrate after adding results.
